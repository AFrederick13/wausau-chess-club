All ages and skill levels are welcome to the Wausau Chess Club. There are zero fees or memberships required, just show up ready to watch or play! Rest assured, we maintain a casual and friendly playing enviorment. We will provide boards and clocks, but you may bring your own set as well. Below you can see our reoccuring meeting locations, days, and times for every week. If you have any questions at all, feel free to contact us directly.
